HOW TO PUT YOUR WEBSITE ONLINE FOR FREE
Dostana House Lifting Shifting
==================================================

You have one file: index.html  (this is the whole website - text, design,
images/icons and contact buttons are all inside it)

Two ways to publish it. Method A is recommended. Both are free.


--------------------------------------------------
METHOD A - GitHub Pages (free address like
           yourname.github.io)
--------------------------------------------------

What you need: an email address. That's it. No card, no payment.

STEP 1 - Create a GitHub account
  - Go to https://github.com/signup
  - Use your email (neerajpathan9@gmail.com is fine)
  - Pick a username you are happy to show in the web address.
    Example: if you choose "dostana", your site becomes
    https://dostana.github.io
    (Username must be in small letters/numbers, no spaces.)

STEP 2 - Create a repository (a folder on GitHub)
  - After logging in, click the "+" at the top right > "New repository"
  - Repository name:   dostana
    (Any name works. Keep it short, small letters, no spaces.)
  - Set it to "Public"  (must be public for free Pages hosting)
  - Leave everything else as it is
  - Click "Create repository"

STEP 3 - Upload index.html
  - On the new page, click "uploading an existing file"
    (or the "Add file" button > "Upload files")
  - Drag index.html from your phone/computer into the box
  - Click "Commit changes" (green button)

STEP 4 - Turn on the website
  - In the repository, click "Settings" (top menu)
  - In the left sidebar, click "Pages"
  - Under "Build and deployment":
      Source   : Deploy from a branch
      Branch   : main      Folder: / (root)
  - Click "Save"
  - Wait 1 to 3 minutes, then refresh that page.
    A message will appear: "Your site is live at https://..."

STEP 5 - Your address
  - It will be:  https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/dostana/
  - Example:     https://dostana.github.io/dostana/
  - Open it on your phone. Check the Call and WhatsApp buttons work.

TIP - shorter address: if you name the repository exactly
  YOUR-USERNAME.github.io  (example: dostana.github.io), the site opens at
  https://dostana.github.io  with no extra folder at the end.
  To get this, create a NEW repository with that exact name and repeat
  steps 3 and 4.


--------------------------------------------------
METHOD B - Netlify Drop (fastest, about 2 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------

  - Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop
  - Drag index.html into the page
  - You get a live link immediately (something.netlify.app)
  - Sign up free (email or Google) to keep the link permanently
    and to rename it, e.g. dostana.netlify.app
  - Netlify's free plan allows a custom domain later if you buy one.

CHANGING THE SITE LATER
--------------------------------------------------

On GitHub:
  - Open the repository > click index.html > click the pencil (Edit) icon
  - Make your change > "Commit changes"
  - The live site updates in about a minute

Things you will probably want to change first:
  - The wording in the "Services" cards (add or remove what you offer)
  - The service area - right now it says "across Haryana". You can list
    your cities: "Rohtak, Jhajjar, Sonipat, Panipat..."
  - Add photos of your own work. Ask me and I will add a gallery section
    with your photos.
  - Prices: a "Starting from" line, if you want to show rates.
  - The YouTube video in the "Watch our work" section (see below).


--------------------------------------------------
ADDING OR CHANGING VIDEOS
--------------------------------------------------

The page now has a "Watch our work" section with one embedded video
and buttons for your channel and a Subscribe link.

To change which video is embedded:
  - Open the video on YouTube. The link looks like
      https://youtu.be/TBlEUnLr49I?si=Rgx-oIM5dyh_avGY
    The code after "youtu.be/" (here TBlEUnLr49I) is the video ID.
  - In index.html, find this line:
      src="https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/TBlEUnLr49I?rel=0"
  - Replace TBlEUnLr49I with the new video ID. Keep the rest as it is.
  - Also update the two "Watch this video" links if you want them to
    point to the same video.

The page also shows two video cards under the player ("More from the
channel"):
  - A house moved five feet      -> video ID 0xb7BtmmRzY
  - Is a degree worth it?        -> video ID X4gFMstjiPk
Each card is a link that opens the video on YouTube. To swap a card,
change its ID in BOTH places: the link (youtu.be/ID) and the thumbnail
(i.ytimg.com/vi/ID/hqdefault.jpg).

To add a third card, copy one whole <a class="vcard"> ... </a> block in
index.html and change the ID, the heading and the one-line text. Or send
me the links and I will add them.

Note: the channel link and Subscribe button already point to
  https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC7iXg6gFdiD1MVeqq3jYjyg
If you ever move to a custom handle like youtube.com/@dostana, tell me
and I will update all three links.

--------------------------------------------------
GETTING FOUND ON GOOGLE (IMPORTANT)
--------------------------------------------------

A new site does NOT appear on Google the day it goes live. Google has
to find it, read it, and then decide to show it. Two things speed this up.

STEP 1 - Upload three files to the repository
  index.html, robots.txt and sitemap.xml  ->  all three at the top
  level of the repo (not inside a folder).

STEP 2 - Tell Google the site exists (Google Search Console, free)
  - Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
  - Sign in with your Google account
  - Choose "URL prefix" and paste your full link:
      https://neeraj-cyber891.github.io/dostana-house-lifting-shifting/
  - For verification choose the HTML file method. Google gives you a
    file with a name like google1a2b3c4d5e6f.html - upload it to the
    same repository (same place as index.html), wait a minute, then
    click Verify.
  - Once verified, open "Sitemaps" in the left menu, type
      sitemap.xml
    and click Submit.
  - Then open "URL Inspection", paste your page link and click
    "Request indexing".

  Normally the page starts showing in Google search in a few days to
two weeks after this. Searching your exact business name will work
first. Words like "house lifting Sirsa" are competitive and take
longer - that is normal, not a fault in the site.

STEP 3 - Google Business Profile (this is the big one for local work)
  - Go to https://business.google.com and create a free profile for
    Dostana House Lifting Shifting
  - Add your phone numbers, the website link, your service area
    (Sirsa, Hanumangarh, Bhadra, Haryana, Punjab) and photos
  - This is what makes you appear in Google Maps and in the "near me"
    results at the top of the page - usually faster and more useful
    than the website alone for a local service business

Other things that help: put the link on your WhatsApp Business profile,
share it in local Facebook/WhatsApp groups, and ask happy customers to
mention the business name online. Google trusts a site that other
places link to.


--------------------------------------------------
THINGS TO KNOW
--------------------------------------------------

  - A free GitHub Pages site is public: anyone with the link can see it.
    Do not put anything private on it.
  - GitHub Pages does not give you a contact form. That is why this page
    uses Call / WhatsApp / Email buttons - these work reliably and cost
    nothing. If you want a form later, a free service like Formspree can
    be added.
  - Test the page on a phone before sharing the link with customers.
  - Share the link on WhatsApp Business, Google Business Profile and your
    vehicle/board so people can find it.
