What we do

Services

From a single household to a full building, the work is planned, packed and moved by one team.

House shifting

Full household relocation — furniture, appliances, kitchen goods and personal items packed, loaded and set down at your new address.

House & structure lifting

Raising an existing house or structure where the level needs to change — planned with proper supports before any lifting begins.

Office & shop relocation

Desks, files, stock and equipment moved with the layout of the new premises in mind, so you can reopen quickly.

Packing & loading

Careful wrapping of fragile items, cartons and covers for furniture, plus loading and unloading by the crew.

Transport

Vehicles arranged according to the size of the move, with the goods accompanied from pickup to delivery.

Heavy & odd items

Safes, machinery, almirahs, pianos and other awkward loads that need extra hands and the right lifting gear.
